TMJ PAIN

The temperomandibular joint (TMJ) is the joint that connects your mandible (lower jaw) to your skull. The joint is found on both sides of your head in front of your ears. It allows your jaw to open and close, enabling you to speak and eat.

SIGNS AND SYMPTOM

TMJ disorders, also known as temperomandibular joint dysfunction (TMD), are a group of conditions that affect the TMJ and the surrounding muscles and ligaments. These disorders can cause a variety of symptoms.

Symptoms Includes:

Jaw pain
Jaw clicking and popping
Pain while eating
Ear pain
Ear ringing & stuffiness
Pain in/around eyes
Tightness in head
Headaches/dizziness
Stiff or sore jaw muscles
Pain in the temple area
Locking of the jaw joint
If you see any such symptoms, it is suggested to visit the best TMJ Specialist in Chennai for the immediate solution.
TMJ issues can either be a joint problem that leads to clicking sounds, or a muscle problem which leads to pain in the facial region.

What Are Risk Factors for TMJ Syndrome?

There are a number of factors that can increase your risk of developing TMJ syndrome, including:

Gender:

Women are more likely to develop TMJ syndrome than men.

Age:

TMJ syndrome is more common in adults between the ages of 20 and 40.

Genetics:

Some people may be more genetically predisposed to developing TMJ syndrome.

Trauma:

A jaw injury can increase your risk of developing TMJ syndrome.

Pain tolerance:

People who are more sensitive to pain may be more likely to develop TMJ syndrome.

Occupational factors:

People who clench or grind their teeth, or who have jobs that involve a lot of jaw movement, may be more likely to develop TMJ syndrome.

Stress:

When stressed out, your facial muscles are contracted, which leads to muscle spasms in the face, consequently causing trigger points in those muscles. The pain from these trigger points are transferred to other areas, which causes headaches and facial pain, akin to migraines.
