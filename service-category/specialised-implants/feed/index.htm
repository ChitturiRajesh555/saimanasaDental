Specialised Implants

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. They are a permanent solution for missing teeth and can provide a number of benefits, including:

A natural-looking smile:

Dental implants look and feel just like natural teeth.

Improved chewing ability:

Dental implants can help you to chew food more effectively.

Increased confidence:

Dental implants can help you to feel more confident about your smile.

Dental implants are a safe and effective procedure, and the success rate is very high. However, there are some risks associated with dental implants, including:

Infection:

There is a small risk of infection at the implant site.

Gum recession:

Over time, the gums may recede around the implant.

Failure:

In rare cases, an implant may fail.
If you are considering dental implants, it is important to talk to your dentist about the risks and benefits of this procedure.

Here are some of the most common questions about dental implants:

What are the advantages of dental implants over other types of tooth replacement?

Dental implants have a number of advantages over other types of tooth replacement, including:

They are more stable than dentures.
They can help to preserve bone density.
They look and feel more natural than bridges.
They can last for many years, if they are properly cared for.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last for many years, if they are properly cared for. However, the lifespan of an implant can vary depending on a number of factors, including the patient’s overall health and the quality of the implant.

What is the cost of dental implants?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on a number of factors, including the number of implants needed, the type of implant, and the dentist’s fees. However, dental implants are generally a more expensive option than other types of tooth replacement.

What is the surgery like for dental implants?

The surgery to place dental implants is usually performed under local anesthesia. The procedure takes about 30-60 minutes per implant. After the surgery, you may experience some pain and swelling, but this usually goes away within a few days.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Once your dental implants are in place, you will need to care for them just like you would your natural teeth. This includes brushing and flossing them regularly. You should also see your dentist for regular checkups and cleanings.
If you are considering dental implants, talk to your dentist about whether they are right for you. Dental implants can provide a number of benefits, including a natural-looking smile, improved chewing ability, and increased confidence.
